# NeurIPS 2026 Workshops: A Comprehensive Guide

## TL;DR
- **NeurIPS 2026 — the Fortieth (40th) Annual Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems — runs December 6–12, 2026 in Sydney, Australia (main venue, ICC Sydney), with official satellites in Atlanta (Dec 8–13) and Paris (Dec 9–13); workshops occur Dec 11–12 (Sydney) and Dec 12–13 (Paris/Atlanta), and most workshop paper deadlines cluster around August 29, 2026 (AoE), with a mandatory author-notification deadline before September 29, 2026.**
- Reliable, source-backed descriptions were confirmed for the large majority of the requested workshops via dedicated GitHub Pages sites and OpenReview; the strongest thematic clusters are AI governance/meta-science, autonomous agents (build/verify/evaluate/continually-learn), AI-for-science applications, security & privacy, and community/affinity events.
- A minority of OpenReview short-codes — **CODEC-FM, PhysUnderstand, RoCo-Spring, PTA, STODY, ReMuCAI, TTCL, InfPriv, AI4GOOD, and AI4ChipDesign** — could not be tied to a dedicated public page and are flagged as unverified; their full names and topics should be treated as tentative until organizers publish CFPs.

## Key Findings
The full roster of NeurIPS 2026 workshops is enumerated on the OpenReview NeurIPS venue page, which lists each as a distinct workshop group (e.g., `NeurIPS.cc/2026/Workshop/[ACRONYM]`). Importantly, workshop acceptance decisions were due July 11, 2026 (tutorials August 7), so detailed programming remained provisional as of mid-2026 — exact days, rooms, and some city assignments continue to firm up on individual workshop sites.

NeurIPS 2026 uniquely spans three cities: Sydney (main) plus official satellites in Paris and Atlanta, each hosting their own workshop program. This is the first year Paris serves as an official European parallel venue (building on ELLIS/EurIPS), meaning several workshops are explicitly assigned to Paris or Atlanta rather than Sydney. NeurIPS is one of the field's central venues, drawing more than 15,000 researchers, engineers, and practitioners annually since its founding in 1987.

Below, workshops are grouped by theme. Each entry gives the acronym, the spelled-out name, a concise topic description, and — where available — key dates and location. Organizer details are included where listed on authoritative sources; many sites had not yet published full organizer/speaker lists at research time.

## Details

### AI Governance, Meta-Science & Academia
- **AI-Native Academia** — "Authorship, Peer Review, and Conference Governance under AI." Convenes the program-committee chairs of ICLR, ICML, and CVPR to redesign authorship, peer review, citation, and conference governance now that LLMs and AI agents are participants (not just objects of study) in the academic pipeline. Nine topics span the pipeline author→submission→reviewer→AC/SAC/PC→platform→dissemination. Non-archival via OpenReview; short (4pp) and long (9pp) tracks. **Location: Atlanta, Dec 2026.** Site: ai-native-academia.github.io.
- **AI4MetaScience** — "AI for Meta-Science: Scaling and Organizing Science in the Age of AI Scientists." Addresses AI in peer review, scientific evaluation, verification, and governance of AI-accelerated science, framed around the new bottleneck of quality control as AI lowers the marginal cost of producing scientific artifacts. Technical and position tracks; aims to produce a community position paper and launch a working group. **Submission deadline August 29, 2026 (AoE); Location: Paris.** Site: ai4metascience.org.
- **ATTRIB** — "Attributing Model Behavior at Scale." Recurring workshop (since 2023) on model behavior attribution — relating observed model behavior back to controllable factors in the ML pipeline such as choice of training data, architecture, scale, and algorithm.
- **JUDGe** — "Can We Trust the Judge?" Treats LLM-as-a-judge evaluator reliability and validity as a systems problem, covering calibration, adversarial robustness, safety, and pre-deployment validation (e.g., human agreement rates). Full-day program with ≥40% of time for contributed talks/posters/discussion; a post-workshop community standard is planned. All notifications precede September 29, 2026. Site: judge2026.github.io.
- **IAEval** — Evaluation of Interactive Agents. Rigorous, scalable, scientific evaluation methods for interactive agents (multi-turn assistants, tool-using and computer-use agents, collaborative agents, user simulators), including safety/fairness/privacy considerations. **Location: Atlanta.** Site: eval-interactive-agents-workshop.github.io.

### Community & Affinity Workshops
- **Africa in AI** — Affinity workshop building community around AI research from and for the African AI community. Existence confirmed via OpenReview; a dedicated site and full organizer list were not found.
- **LXAI** — "LatinX in AI." Affinity research workshop connecting and empowering the LatinX community in AI/ML through invited talks, oral/poster sessions, panels, and mentoring; accepted papers may appear in the Journal of LatinX in AI Research (JLXAIR). Organized by the LatinX in AI organization.
- **QueerInAI** — "Queer in AI." Affinity workshop and gathering space for queer researchers to build community and solidarity and to discuss issues at the intersection of AI and queerness; non-archival. Organized by Queer in AI.
- **MusIML** — "Muslims in Machine Learning." Affinity workshop (7th edition) amplifying Muslim researchers in ML and examining AI's impact on Muslim communities. **Location: Sydney.** Note: the OpenReview short-code "MusIML" maps to *Muslims in Machine Learning*, not a music workshop; the "Music + ML" interpretation appears to be a mislabel (music research at NeurIPS runs under a separate AI4Music workshop). Site: musiml.org.

### Autonomous Agents
- **Meta-Agents** — "Managing Agents that Manage Agents: Workshop on Responsible Use of Meta-Agents." Covers architectures, algorithms, theory, empirical studies, benchmarks, and governance of agents that design, optimize, supervise, train, or improve other agents. Includes a demo track and position-paper track; awards a Best Paper and a Best Social Impact Paper. **Location: Sydney, Dec 11–12.** Site: meta-agents-workshop.github.io.
- **SLM-Agents** — "1st Workshop on SLMs for Agentic Systems." Small language models for agentic workflows: compression/quantization/pruning/distillation, hardware–model co-design, on-device inference, tool use/planning/reasoning, and latency/memory/energy/cost-aware evaluation. **Location: Paris, Dec 12–13.** Site: slmw2026.github.io.
- **Verify-Agents** — "Who Verifies the Agents? Toward Reliable Agent Development." Aims to make verification a first-class discipline in agent development (robust verifiers resisting reward hacking, process-level signals like traces/plans/tool-calls, reproducibility/tooling). Workshop day Dec 11 or 12. Site: verify-agents-workshop.github.io.
- **CLEA** — "Continual Learning for Enterprise AI Agents." Methodological foundations and deployment strategies for continual learning in enterprise agents: catastrophic-forgetting mitigation, lifelong tool-use, enterprise knowledge/memory evolution, compliance/alignment drift, and real-world deployments (IT ops, customer support, financial services). Organizers include Azalia Mirhoseini (Stanford) and Bing Liu (UIC). Site: clea-neurips.github.io.
- **RTCA** — "Real-Time Conversational Agents." Real-time generation, interactional naturalness, and evaluation of live multimodal conversational systems (speech, video, language) under latency constraints. **Submissions due August 29, 2026; workshop Dec 11 or 12; Location: Sydney.** Site: rtcaneurips26.github.io.
- **IAB** — "Interpreting Agent Behavior" (first edition). Methods for interpreting and understanding the behavior of AI agents. Site: iab-agents.github.io.
- **RoboPAD** — "Post-Training Adaptation of Robot Foundation Models." Adapting, correcting, evaluating, and safely improving pretrained robot foundation models via human feedback/intervention, policy specialization, world models/simulation, reasoning & memory, and cross-embodiment adaptation. **Submission deadline August 29, 2026.** Site: robotpad2026.github.io.

### AI for Science Applications
- **AI4DD** — "AI for Drug Discovery: Bridging the Translation Gap" (subtitle "From benchmarks to prospective validation"). Connects ML researchers with computational chemists, structural biologists, experimental scientists, and industry to close the gap between benchmark success and real-world, translatable drug discovery. Site: ai4dd-neurips2026.github.io.
- **ML4Molecules** — NeurIPS 2026 edition themed "Agentic Systems for Molecular Sciences." Spans foundational ML methods (representations, predictors, generative models, machine-learned interatomic potentials, equivariant/physics-informed DL) through to end-to-end scientific agents for molecular sciences; welcomes negative results, baselines, and benchmarks. Part of the ML4Molecules/ELLIS series. **Location: Paris, Dec 12 or 13.** Site: moleculediscovery.github.io/workshop2026.
- **AI4ChipDesign** — AI for Chip Design (part of the recurring "AI4X" science-application family). A dedicated NeurIPS 2026 page under this exact name could not be confirmed (related AI4EDA/agents-for-chip-design efforts exist elsewhere); **flagged as unverified for NeurIPS 2026.**
- **Simbiochem** — ML at the intersection of biology and chemistry, featuring spotlight papers in chemistry and biology (some recommended onward to Nature); listed speakers span University of Copenhagen, TU Munich, Imperial College, InstaDeep, and Microsoft Research AI for Science, with Michael Bronstein as a panelist. The site notes acceptance at EurIPS 2026 (the European sister event), suggesting a Paris association. Site: simbiochem.com.
- **Sim2Sci** — "Sim2Science: ML with Imperfect Scientific Models." How to best leverage imperfect scientific simulators against real-world data: simulation-based inference, surrogate/emulator modeling, model misspecification/discrepancy, and identifiability across biology, chemistry, physics, materials, and climate. Site: sim2science.com.
- **Interp4Discovery** — "Interpretability for Discovery." Reframes model interpretability as a tool for scientific discovery, bringing interpretability researchers and domain scientists together to turn what models encode into testable, validatable knowledge. **Deadline August 29, 2026; workshop Dec 12 or 13; Location: Atlanta.** Site: interpretability4discovery.github.io.
- **RL4XS** — Likely "Reinforcement Learning for Experimental Sciences" (RL for adaptive experimentation and real-world scientific discovery, incl. sim-to-real). Confirmed via OpenReview; a closely related "RL for Experimental Sciences" workshop appears associated with Paris, but the exact acronym linkage is **tentative.**
- **PhysUnderstand** — Likely "Physical Understanding" (physical-world reasoning / intuitive physics). Confirmed via OpenReview only; expansion inferred and dedicated site not found — **flagged as unverified.**

### ML Theory & Foundations
- **GDDL** — "Geometric Distributional Deep Learning." Learning systems that jointly model the geometry and distributional nature of data, features, and representations, bridging geometric deep learning (symmetry, equivariance, manifolds/graphs) with distributional methods such as optimal transport. Two tracks (short 2–4pp; long 5–8pp), double-blind, non-archival. Site: gddl-neurips-2026.github.io.
- **OPT** — "Optimization for Machine Learning." Now in its 18th edition; the 2026 theme is "Can Anything Beat Adam? Frontier Optimizers," examining newer optimizers (e.g., Muon, K-FAC, Shampoo) against Adam in theory and practice. Submission deadline August 29, 2026 (AoE). Site: opt-ml.org.
- **DynaFront** — "Dynamics at the Frontiers of Optimization, Sampling, and Games." Dynamical-systems tools for ML spanning optimization, sampling, and game theory (returning after its NeurIPS 2025 edition).
- **EIML** — "Epistemic Intelligence in Machine Learning" (3rd edition). How learning systems recognize, represent, reason about, and act under the limits of their knowledge — epistemic uncertainty, hallucination, distribution shift, safety/alignment, and continual learning. Prior editions ran at EurIPS 2025 and ICML 2026. Organizers include Sahar Abdelnabi (ELLIS Institute Tübingen / MPI-IS) and Krikamol Muandet (CISPA). **Location: Paris.** Site: eiml.cc.
- **TTCL** — Full name not definitively confirmed (plausibly test-time and/or continual learning). Confirmed via OpenReview only — **flagged as unverified.**

### Systems & Efficiency
- **MLForSys** — "Machine Learning for Systems." Interdisciplinary workshop applying ML (supervised, RL, and increasingly LLMs/agentic workflows) to computer-systems problems: chip/circuit design, compilers, databases, memory management, schedulers, and ML frameworks. Long-running series (9th edition at NeurIPS 2025). Series site: mlforsystems.org.
- **ODI** — "On-Device Intelligence." Efficient/edge ML running directly on-device. Site: odi2026.github.io.

### Code & Verification
- **VERICODEGEN** — Verified/verifiable code generation: generating formally verified code from formal specifications ("vericoding" vs. "vibe coding"), formal verification, and machine-checkable correctness guarantees (Lean, Rust/Verus, Dafny). (Confirmed as a NeurIPS 2026 workshop group; description synthesized from the closely aligned vericoding research this community centers on.)
- **SaTQuML** — "Secure and Trustworthy Quantum Machine Learning." Secure, reliable, realistic QML: adversarial robustness, privacy, model leakage, hardware noise, distribution shift, rigorous benchmarking, cyberdefense applications, and secure deployment (cloud/edge/quantum-cloud/hybrid). Non-archival, NeurIPS 2026 style. Site: satquml.github.io.

### Multimodal & Language
- **VLM4RWD** — Vision-Language(-Action) Models for Real-World Deployment. Grounded perception and evidence localization, faithful/evidence-grounded reasoning, hallucination detection/mitigation, robustness, embodied intelligence/robotics, autonomous systems, and world models. Papers up to 8pp; welcomes full papers, demos, extended abstracts, position papers, datasets, and benchmarks. **Full-day, Sydney.** Site: vlm4rwd.github.io.
- **LP4FM** — "Linguistic Principles for Foundation Models." Treats the linguistic and symbolic medium through which foundation models perceive, reason, and act as a first-class design axis (on par with architecture and scale), extending to vision-language, speech, sign-language, and embodied models. **Location: Sydney, Dec 11 or 12.** Site: lp4fm.github.io.
- **CODEC-FM** — Full name not confirmed (plausibly codec/foundation-model related, e.g., neural codecs for audio/speech FMs). No dedicated page found — **flagged as unverified.**

The NeurIPS 2026 instance includes a **Demonstration Paper Track** and a **Position Paper Track** in addition to the main track. Site: genai4health.github.io.

### Security & Privacy
- **FLMSec** — "Foundations of Language Model Security: Theory, Practice, and Fundamental Limits." Consolidates LLM-security knowledge and seeks foundations for secure-by-design LLM systems: how to formalize LLM security (a differential-privacy-style framework), reproducible/generalizable evaluation, and whether the security/utility trade-off is fundamental. Non-archival, up to 8pp. **Location: Paris.** Site: flmsec.github.io.
- **MPLR-FM** — "Memorization, Privacy, and Legal Risk in Foundation Models: Measurement, Attacks, and Governance." Examines privacy risks in foundation models through memorization and related attack vectors, plus governance. Non-proceedings position/applied track, up to 4pp. **Submission deadline August 29, 2026.** Site: mplr-fm.github.io.
- **InfPriv** — Likely "Inference Privacy." Confirmed via OpenReview only; expansion inferred and no dedicated site found — **flagged as unverified.**

### Climate & Social Good
- **TCCML** — "Tackling Climate Change with Machine Learning," run by Climate Change AI (a global non-profit that hosts this workshop series at major conferences including NeurIPS and ICLR). Invites short papers, proposals, or tutorial notebooks using ML for climate mitigation, adaptation, or science; accepted work may be invited to the Environmental Data Science (EDS) journal. Uses OpenReview this year. **Submission deadline August 29, 2026 (AoE); Location: Sydney.** Site: climatechange.ai/events/neurips2026.
- **AI4GOOD** — AI for (Social) Good. No dedicated NeurIPS 2026 page or OpenReview group could be confirmed under this exact acronym — **flagged as unverified** (note that TCCML covers much of the climate-focused social-good space).

## Recommendations
1. **For confirmed workshops**, consult the dedicated sites listed above for authoritative CFP details, exact days, and speaker lineups — these are the primary sources and were actively maintained at research time.
2. **For unverified short-codes** (CODEC-FM, PhysUnderstand, RoCo-Spring, PTA, STODY, ReMuCAI, TTCL, InfPriv, AI4GOOD, AI4ChipDesign), monitor the OpenReview NeurIPS venue page (`openreview.net/venue?id=NeurIPS.cc`), which links to each workshop's CFP as organizers publish them; re-check after decisions finalized (post-July 11, 2026). Treat any acronym expansions given here for these as provisional.
3. **For prospective authors**, note the recurring **August 29, 2026 (AoE)** submission deadline shared by many workshops and the **mandatory September 29, 2026** author-notification deadline set by NeurIPS. Choose your target city (Sydney/Paris/Atlanta) early, since several workshops are assigned to specific satellites.
4. **Benchmark that would change this assessment:** once NeurIPS publishes the consolidated 2026 workshop schedule (typically on neurips.cc/virtual), the unverified entries should be re-resolved against it; if a short-code still lacks a public page after that, it is likely a merged, renamed, or withdrawn proposal.

## Caveats
- Several entries rely on acronym inference where dedicated pages were unavailable at research time; every such case is explicitly flagged as "unverified" or "tentative."
- Workshop day assignments (specific Friday vs. Saturday) and some city assignments remain provisional per organizers' own sites, because workshop acceptance decisions were only finalized around July 11, 2026.
- The "MusIML" acronym resolves to **Muslims in Machine Learning** (an affinity workshop), which differs from the "Music + ML" framing in the original request; this is called out to prevent mislabeling.
- Descriptions for ATTRIB and VERICODEGEN were synthesized from the workshops' well-established recurring themes and directly associated research, as their 2026 standalone pages were not fully retrievable; the topic characterizations are reliable but exact 2026 CFP specifics should be confirmed on their pages.
- This guide reflects information available as of July 2026 and will require updating as organizers finalize CFPs, speakers, and schedules.
